Welcome Message — Dear Friends & Colleagues Prof. Aly Azmy & Prof. Nivine Gado

ASU-ICOC 2026 is dedicated to delivering high quality education, providing clinical knowledge and skills training, promoting multi-disciplinary and multi-specialty care, acting as a platform for networking and generating interest in research.

The scientific program of ASU-ICOC 2026 aims to discuss the recent advances and emerging trends in the management and treatment of common problems in all branches of oncology introducing more focused sessions that will feature cutting edge presentations, special panel discussions and livelier interaction with leaders and experts all over the world.

ASU-ICOC 2026 is designed to provide oncologists with the latest evidence-based strategies and exchange new data, create a unique and intellectually stimulating environment to optimize care and outcomes for patients with cancer. We look forward to welcoming you and hope that you find ASU-ICOC 2026 to be an exciting and valuable educational experience.
